High Net Worth Individuals Law

We have always focused our work on advising successful people on every aspect of their lives and interests.

Those with high net worth and complex assets may encounter issues with non-disclosure in an attempt to conceal the true financial position. It is vitally important to have legal advisers with the experience, expertise and connections to help you secure the best possible outcome.

Our HNWI lawyers at Hassan Luk understand that wealthy individuals need to balance the demands of business, investment and protecting their wealth at all times and often in the public eye. We save you time and money and provide you with reassurance that your best interests are being looked after by us. We report to you regularly about the progress of your case and monitor costs for you. We provide you with a legal health check review and identify areas in your personal, professional and business lives that may need addressing.

Our legal services for high-net worth individuals (HNWIs)

When a couple divorces, one of the most complex and important tasks they must undertake is the division of shared finances and property. This is especially true for high-net-worth couples, who often have numerous assets and liabilities to consider.

A high-net-worth divorce can be a very delicate matter, which is why it is so important to seek legal representation from a specialist family law lawyer.

Specialist high-net-worth lawyers at Hassan Luk offer clients discretion and confidentiality.

There are occasions when discretion is key to an individual or a business. The HNW Team at Hassan Luk can remove that initial first contact with third party suppliers for you ensuring total anonymity in the first instance. We act responsively to our clients’ needs and understand when discretion is paramount.
