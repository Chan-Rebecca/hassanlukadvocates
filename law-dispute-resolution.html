Dispute Resolution Law

Disputes come in all shapes and sizes, and often when you least expect them. Our expert lawyers are on hand to overtun any legal challenges you might face.

Disputes are a fact of modern life. Understanding and dealing with a dispute in a professional and commercial way has never been more important. If you are in a dispute with a business or individual you may need the support of our dispute resolution lawyers.

Our lawyers will provide pragmatic advice regarding your dispute including whether alternative dispute resolution may be appropriate. Our litigation experts cover a range of disputes, including commercial, family and property as well as medical negligence. Many of our lawyers are also specialists in particular sectors, such as construction, tax, employment and technology.

How we can protect your business

Should litigation be inevitable, our team will ensure that you have the most effective representation possible. We also have extensive experience in resolving international commercial disputes and our Arbitration team have the experience and knowledge of the rules at various international arbitral institutions.

Our legal services for business disputes

We understand that corporate and commercial disputes can significantly impact your business operations and relationships. Our experienced litigation team is dedicated to resolving complex conflicts efficiently and effectively, always prioritising your commercial objectives.

The cost of a defamation or slander case is not only financial, but can also cost you time and damage your reputation. Contact us and we will work with focus and dedication to protect your position.

If you’re involved in a dispute over property, including disputes with a landlord or a tenant, our expert team can help you reach a quick and successful resolution.

If you have suffered financial or other losses because of bad or incorrect advice from a professional advisor such as an accountant or an advocate, contact us for free advice.

Will, inheritance and trust disputes are often stressful and upsetting, particularly because they usually involve members of the same family.

Our expert lawyers can help you to contest a Will or defend a claim if someone else is challenging the Will.

The payment of tax affects every business and individual, but navigating KRA and its processes can be complicated. Our team have direct experience of working within KRA and can help you reach a satisfactory agreement for your business and KRA as well as helping you to build a solid working relationship going forward.

Given the complexities of construction projects it is unlikely that any project will reach practical completion without some issues arising during the course of construction works.

Primarily the main reasons which can give rise to a dispute involving construction contracts comprise of:

  • Disruption and delay on a particular project which jeopardises the contractual completion date
  • Payment disputes
  • Defects arising in the design and/or workmanship
  • Parties terminating the contract
  • Final account disputes

Technology disputes and disruption to services from cyber-attacks, can seriously impact business operations and brand reputations. Our team utilises senior expertise in the technology and telecoms sectors to advise clients on disputes arising from AI, blockchain, cryptocurrency, ecommerce, cloud computing and hardware issues.

Navigating regulatory investigations can be daunting, especially as authorities increasingly scrutinise compliance with financial, environmental, or sector-specific regulations. Our team assists businesses in responding to investigations, defending against enforcement actions, and managing compliance concerns, minimising the disruption to your business.

Navigating employment disputes requires a delicate balance of legal expertise and commercial acumen. Our specialist team understand the complex and often sensitive nature of these issues, which can significantly impact your business operations and workplace culture.

When facing issues of fraud or needing to recover assets, swift and decisive action is crucial. Our experienced team provides strategic guidance and representation to protect your interests and secure your assets.

In today’s complex business environment, alternative dispute resolution (ADR) often provides a more efficient and cost-effective approach to resolving conflicts. At Hassanluk, we offer expert guidance in various ADR methods, including mediation, helping you find solutions outside of traditional litigation. See our Arbitration services here.

What can you expect from us as your dispute resolution lawyers?

Our approachable legal experts have a wealth of knowledge and experience to draw upon. In most cases, civil disputes can be resolved out of court but, if negotiation and mediation should fail to reach an agreement, you can rest assured that our litigation specialists will strive to achieve your best possible outcome in court.

We’ll guide you through the court process, cutting through the jargon to ensure you know where you stand at every step, and represent you and your best interests in the courtroom.

You are always our top priority

At Hassan Luk LLP, we are on your side whatever lies at the heart of the matter with clear, straightforward, sensible advice to help resolve the issue quickly and cost-effectively. We will keep you up-to-date with our progress and make sure you understand your options at every stage along the way.

We will put your best interests first

If you would like advice about a dispute you are currently facing or are worried about a situation that you feel may be getting out of hand, contact our team in confidence – you can be sure of a well-informed, high-quality personal legal service based on integrity, loyalty, commitment and experience.

Our litigation strategy is grounded in practical and commercial solutions. Whether it’s through proactive negotiation or robust litigation strategy, we ensure your business interests are protected every step of the way. With a reputation for success and a client-first approach, Hassan Luk is your trusted partner in resolving commercial disputes.
