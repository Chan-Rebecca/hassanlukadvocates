Real Estate Tax

Our lawyers advise regularly on the direct and indirect tax treatment of real estate transactions for buyers, investors, sellers, landlords and occupiers of commercial and residential property.

When you need advice on Real Estate taxes, you need to rely on experts who can think commercially. We advise on a wide range of real estate tax issues, and offer exceptionally high levels of service to get you the guidance that will make a difference.

This includes areas such as Stamp Duty Tax, where the rules change frequently and have become increasingly complex. It is important to check if any particular acquisition will qualify for reduced stamp duty rates. We also cover VAT on both commercial and residential property.

Our experience in banking and finance transactions enables us to advise on how lenders and borrowers are affected by real estate tax. In addition to the usual domestic issues, we have experience of cross border matters, such as withholding tax on paying interest to a person outside Kenya and how to avoid a withholding pursuant to a double tax treaty.

Common issues we solve for our clients :

  • Construction industry scheme. Whilst this primarily affects payments under a building contract, it may arise in other circumstances
  • Banking and finance transactions
  • Stamp Duty Land Tax
  • VAT on both commercial and residential property
  • Capital Gains Tax, including CGT for foreigners
  • The implications of paying rent to a landlord that is outside Kenya
  • The significant tax changes regarding high value residential property that have taken place over the last few years
  • How tax issues affect charities, especially in transactional and development matters. We have experience of direct tax and charities.

Who have our lawyers worked with?

  • Construction companies
  • Banks
  • Landlords and tenants
  • Occupiers
  • Developers
  • Charities
