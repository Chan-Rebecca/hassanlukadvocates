FinTech Law

With our deep knowledge of the FinTech sector, our lawyers deliver focussed advice while mitigating risks and supporting growth.

As the FinTech sector continues its rapid growth, businesses have unprecedented opportunities to transform the way they engage with money, how they pay, and get paid, for their online and offline products and services.

From digital finance and artificial intelligence to machine learning algorithms, blockchain, and automation, these innovations are revolutionising financial and payment services and enhancing accessibility. However, being able to navigate the complex regulatory landscape and related operational, data and cost issues, and overcome the challenge is essential to business success. You need the right legal partner for the journey.

Our FinTech sector services

Helping you raise money for your business at every stage of business growth.

Helping you develop and implement your lending or payments platform and solutions to customers or negotiating the terms of a facility with a bank or other lender to grow your business.

Negotiating a wide range of commercial payment agreements, including merchant acquiring and aggregation of alternative payment and gateway agreements; open banking; card scheme incentive arrangements, referral and reseller contracts and SaaS supplier agreements to build fintech platforms and infrastructure.

Ensuring your assets are protected for growth. Visit our Data Protection page.

From designing and implementing employee share schemes, providing general employment law advice to drafting employment contracts, policies and procedures.

Working internationally to support you on all aspects of your regulatory journey, including financial services and payment and privacy compliance.

Navigating regulatory compliance in financial services and payments

The FinTech sector faces a significant challenge in complying with an ever-changing regulatory landscape. As businesses often operate in hyper-scale mode, raising capital and addressing legal needs throughout their evolution becomes crucial.

At Hassan Luk LLP, we leverage our extensive experience in the financial and payment services industry (both from a provider and merchant perspective) to provide a comprehensive range of legal services for growing businesses. We have worked with FinTech companies at all stages of growth, from start-ups to established enterprises, as well as major online and offline merchant businesses.

Our understanding of the start-up ecosystem and expertise in managing regulatory challenges, emerging technologies, and consumer patterns means we have the right tools and experience to support your business.
