We provide legal services in every area of law practice :

Core Corporate & Business Law

Private Companies

We guide private businesses through every stage of their lifecycle, handling everything from company formation and day-to-day corporate governance to strategic restructuring and shareholder agreements.

Venture Capital

We connect high-growth startups with investors, structuring financing rounds, equity allocations, and strategic partnerships that fuel innovation and scale businesses safely.

Corporate Tax

We help businesses navigate complex tax landscapes by structuring transactions, managing compliance, and delivering strategic advice to minimize liabilities and maximize profitability.

Bankruptcy

We provide strategic guidance for businesses and individuals facing financial distress, helping navigate debt restructuring, reorganization, or liquidation to secure a viable financial future.

Global & Mobility Services

Immigration

We assist individuals and families navigating the complexities of visas, permanent residency, and citizenship applications, ensuring a smooth and compliant path to their goals.

Business Immigration

We help companies secure the global talent they need by managing corporate visas, employee transfers, and workplace compliance for international workforces.

International Desk

Our cross-border specialists bridge legal and cultural gaps, providing seamless counsel for international clients investing, expanding, or managing disputes across multiple jurisdictions.

Assets, Wealth & Philanthropy

Real Estate

We handle the legal complexities of property ownership, advising clients on commercial and residential transactions, leasing, zoning, development, and real estate financing.

Private Wealth

We provide discrete, comprehensive legal strategies for high-net-worth individuals, focusing on asset protection, cross-border investments, and long-term financial preservation.

Wills and Inheritance

We help individuals protect their legacy and provide for loved ones through meticulous estate planning, trusts, and the smooth, compassionate administration of probate.

Charities

We advise non-profits and philanthropic organizations on formation, tax-exempt status, regulatory compliance, and governance, allowing them to focus entirely on their mission.

Industry Specializations & Personal Advocacy

Natural Resources

We advise clients in the energy, mining, and environmental sectors on regulatory compliance, licensing, land acquisition, and sustainable project development.

Work Injury

We advocate fiercely for employees injured on the job, securing fair workers' compensation benefits, medical coverage, and financial recovery during their rehabilitation.

Criminal Law

We offer robust, strategic defense representation for individuals and corporations facing criminal charges, dedicatedly protecting their rights at every stage of the legal process.
