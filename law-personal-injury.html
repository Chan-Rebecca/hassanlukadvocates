What's classed as an industrial disease?
If you develop an illness through the activities involved in your job, it could be classed as an industrial disease. Examples of an industrial disease include: dermatitis, respiratory illnesses, cancer, infectious diseases such as COVID-19, musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs), noise-induced hearing loss (NIHL), and hand arm vibration syndrome (HAVS), among others.

What classes as a serious injury?
A serious injury is something that will have long-term effects on you or a loved one’s life. It may impact your ability to work, live independently, continue with your hobbies, and in some serious cases, manage your own affairs.

Serious injuries include death or injuries that result in permanent disability, or more than 30 days absence from work. We’ve helped individuals and families after a range of serious injuries, including:

  • Brain injuries
  • Spinal cord injuries
  • Amputation
  • Major trauma including multiple fractures.

We’ve also been there for families after a loved one has died. Our experts have helped them access the support they need and where the person who has died was the main breadwinner, some financial security.
