Legal Advice for Start-Up Businesses

Kickstarting your new business

If you're an ambitious start-up business owner looking for legal support with structuring your business, defining your intellectual property strategy or employment contracts, then our experienced start-up lawyers can help you on your journey.

We help entrepreneurs and founders launch and grow their businesses with the right legal foundations in place.

Kickstarting your new business

Many of our team at Hassan Luk Advocates have been business owners in fast-growth start-ups and scale-ups, so we understand that the legal aspects can often seem onerous and costly. However, having them in place at the outset is critical for scaling your business with ease and avoiding expensive mistakes down the line.

You want your legal obligations to be taken care of so you can focus on building a successful business. At Hassan Luk Advocates, we partner with your start-up business to ensure you invest in the right legal advice and documents, building you a stable legal base.

Delivering specialist business legal advice

As part of our legal solutions, we provide specialist legal advice on:

  • Business structure
  • Investment opportunities
  • Intellectual property
  • Data protection
  • Employment and supplier contracts

Start-up legal packages

Great start-ups are often created by friends, partners or connections with a shared passion or interest. All too often that enthusiasm to take a new idea to market is so intense, you might miss out on one of the most fundamental legal documents: a founders' agreement. Having an agreement up front to align on expectations and have a plan if things change is absolutely key.

We work with founders to ensure they have an agreement in place that will see the business through any changes. This package includes:

  • Consultation session with founders
  • Walkthrough to discuss all key issues including board structure, shareholder rights, vesting of shares, and assignment of IP
  • Creation of a customised founders' agreement

Raising early-stage funding is one of the most important milestones when you are growing your start-up. As well as developing a robust business model and plan, you need to make sure that you have the necessary legal documents in place for investors to consider backing you.

Our start-up lawyers will work with you to make sure you have a suite of documents that makes you investor ready. These include:

  • Shareholder agreements
  • Articles of association
  • Deed of adherence
  • Investor certification
  • Board minutes
  • Shareholder resolutions

Directors' Service Agreements are essential to protect your business as it grows and ensure compliance with your business's obligations under employment legislation.

Our lawyers will work with you to prepare service agreements that meet your business's specific requirements. This package includes:

  • A consultation session with a senior specialist employment lawyer
  • Discussion of key requirements including duties, salary, limits on authority, confidentiality, and notice and termination
  • Creation of a customised directors' service agreement tailored to your business needs

Getting the right employment contracts, policies and procedures in place at the beginning will help your business avoid the pain and expense of putting it right in the future.

Our lawyers will work with you to ensure that you have the right employment documentation. This package includes:

  • A consultation session with a senior specialist employment lawyer
  • Discussion of key employment issues and your business's requirements
  • A template offer of employment letter
  • A template employment contract for general use
  • A staff handbook containing key employment policies and procedures

Getting your business off the ground can often involve hiring individual contractors and consultants at very short notice. To protect your business and avoid misunderstandings and disputes, it is important to put written agreements in place.

Our lawyers will work with you to develop a suitable independent contractors agreement. This package includes:

  • A consultation session with a senior specialist employment lawyer
  • Discussion of key requirements including fees, intellectual property, confidentiality, notice and termination, and employment status
  • Creation of a customised self-employed contractors agreement tailored to your business needs

As start-ups scale and grow, it's important to invest in your people and retain many of those who have contributed to its success. An EMI (Enterprise Management Incentives) option scheme provides employees the right to buy shares in the company in a very tax efficient way.

We work as virtual general counsel for start-up businesses who are going through funding rounds and need ongoing support from a law firm. One of our team of senior lawyers will plug into your business to provide the support you need during this phase of growth, bringing in the right people, the right technology and creating the right legal processes.

We offer this service on a cost-effective fixed monthly fee.
