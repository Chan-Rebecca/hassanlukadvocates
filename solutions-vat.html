VAT & Indirect Tax

VAT and indirect taxes are transaction-driven and complex and can have a significant impact on your bottom line. Complying with the wide range of indirect taxes often requires quick thinking and immediate action.

Our specialist tax lawyers have experience across the full range of indirect tax services, including VAT, customs duties, and stamp duty taxes. With careful planning, we are able to avoid potential pitfalls and reduce the indirect tax risks for a variety of businesses and transactions.

We have broad experience in virtually all types of excise and transaction taxes, including: hospitality, alcohol excise taxes, environmental taxes, fuel taxes, communications and air transportation taxes, heavy vehicle retail taxes, manufacturers taxes, taxes on specified health insurance policies and applicable self-insured health plans.
