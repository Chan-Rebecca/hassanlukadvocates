Procurement Law

Hassan Luk’s specialist team of public procurement lawyers advise both bidders and contracting authorities on challenges arising from public procurements in all sectors.

The procurement process relies on your ability to identify reliable suppliers and maintain lasting relationships. This will not only reduce the risk of delays, defects or disruptions but contribute to the seamless operation of your business. However, supplier management is not always a straightforward process.

With our team of specialist procurement lawyers, we can help you enhance these crucial relationships. From negotiating contracts to structuring bids to navigating regulations, we are dedicated to streamlining the procurement procedure for your business.

Procurement law services offered

We specialise in the procurement of a wide range of technology solutions. From software products to system integrations to cloud solutions, we ensure that your business acquires the highest quality technology.

Our team has experience in the procurement of equipment for major infrastructure projects. By navigating the intricacies of procurement legislation, we can secure essential tools, products and services for your upcoming project.

We advise a number of clients subject to public procurement law, including local government departments and public authorities. Through our experience with various contracting authorities, we can guide you through the complexities of public contracts regulations.

With a thorough understanding of global market dynamics, we can assist with the procurement of business process services nationally and internationally. Whether you require facilities management, human resources support or logistics operations, our team is dedicated to delivering solutions that drive your business towards success.

Managing major projects requires specialised legal expertise to ensure successful execution and compliance. Hassan Luk’s major projects lawyers provide comprehensive legal support for large-scale procurement initiatives.

Our services include contract drafting and negotiation, risk assessment, regulatory compliance, and dispute resolution. We work closely with you to navigate the complexities of major projects, ensuring your interests are protected and objectives are achieved. Trust Hassan Luk to deliver strategic legal solutions tailored to the demands of significant procurement projects.

Navigating the complexities of public procurement requires expert legal guidance. Hassan Luk’s public procurement lawyers offer specialised support for navigating regulatory frameworks and ensuring compliance with procurement laws.
