Legal Operations Consulting

Supporting your Legal Operations

If you're looking to bring efficiency to your in-house legal team to maximise and enhance productivity, reduce costs, streamline processes and focus on value creation, then look no further than our Legal Operations Consulting team.

Supporting your Legal Operations

We specialise in conducting comprehensive analyses of your Legal Operations tools. Our team of Legal Operations consultants work closely with you to assess your current Legal Operations function and collaboratively define the desired state. This allows us to develop a strategic plan tailored to support your legal team in the most effective way.

You want your Legal Operations team to work smarter, not harder. We provide the resources and legal services to optimise your reporting matrix and strategic planning to get your team running smoothly.

Enhancing your Legal Operations

We can help you optimise your Legal Operations providing solutions that:

  • Improve your service delivery to internal stakeholders
  • Streamline your activities by leveraging technology solutions
  • Increase efficiency and productivity within your legal team
  • Optimise team skills and structure for better performance
  • Effectively manage risk and ensure compliance

Contact us today to discover how we can enhance your Legal Operations and drive your legal team's success.

Legal Operations programme

We'll work with you to identify which initiatives you need to consider including in your Legal Operations programme.

Looking to get your service delivery streamlined and standardised? We will review the end-to-end process mapping and standardisation of your legal services. We'll review your legal processes and governance – including contract management and self-serve – to improve the process of engaging and instructing the legal team. We also carry out external counsel instruction enhancements and panel reviews.

You don't need to invest in expensive new technology. We aim to maximise the technology you already have. Our Legal Operations experts help you through the discovery and implementation of sophisticated contract management and document automation software solutions. The result? Your team being able to deliver measurable value to your legal department and business.

You want the best from your legal team. We can help maintain and improve the way they work with an analysis of legal team activities, from granular matters to task and complexity reporting via time recording exercises. We can highlight more efficient work completion and automation solutions including team structure refinement, data analytics, MI/KPI metric and dashboard development.

Your people are your most important asset. Managing them effectively and efficiently with well-being as a priority, is key to the success of the legal team and the business. We can support by optimising legal team meeting coverage, structure and mode of delivery. Our Legal Operations expertise enables you to overhaul and streamline the legal team's new starter onboarding processes, as well as manage team skills analysis and development.

Staying compliant is critical. We provide support with general compliance processes, risk identification and monitoring, claims and dispute reporting.

How can you benefit from a defined Legal Operations programme?

If you're looking to improve productivity and efficiency across the whole business, then a successful Legal Operations programme can do just that.

It can deliver better turn-around times on contracts, including contract value and termination dates. It can improve team satisfaction, as your legal team receives additional support and legal expertise from our team of senior lawyers here at Hassan Luk Advocates. It will help you operationalise and plan for the future.

If you are a legal team looking for strategic planning, additional legal services and project management, contact one of the team at Hassan Luk Advocates so we can discuss tailored Legal Operations solutions for your legal team and your business.

Our Legal Operations experts

Our Legal Operations specialists work with your legal team to streamline your processes and support you in delivering a better experience for your business. They act as an extension of your team to provide additional expertise, knowledge and support.
