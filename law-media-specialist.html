Digital Media Law

We help clients across all sectors of media and entertainment, from individuals to public sector broadcasters.

With the complex nature of contracts, advertising and ownership of content, as well as dealing with the ethical issues of acting in the public interest but respecting privacy, it is important that our clients have an experienced legal partner.

Our media lawyers have worked with both traditional media businesses and disruptive digital media platforms, and are well placed to provide your business with high-quality legal advice.

Our digital media advice

Our team of digital media law experts work with media companies, TV personalities, publishers, authors, music artists, directors, producers, film production companies, galleries and more. Our full service offerings include:

Advising and negotiating TV programming and film development and financial deals, talent agreements, image rights, content licensing and syndication agreements.

In depth understanding of the monetisation of media rights ranging from the negotiation of trading agreements with major agencies, sponsorship agreements, adtech agreements and affiliate marketing deals.

We’ll help you identify the best way to protect and exploit your IP portfolio and shape the most profitable IP strategy for your business. Whatever stage you’re at – we’ll help commercialise your intellectual property so that you take full advantage of your most valuable business assets.

Hassan Luk offers expert guidance in social media law, covering key aspects such as copyright infringement, defamation, privacy breaches, and advertising compliance. Our services include legal advice on social media policy, content creation, user engagement strategies, and navigating platform-specific regulations to ensure that your digital presence is both impactful and legally compliant.
