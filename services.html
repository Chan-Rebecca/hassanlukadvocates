OUR SERVICES

Specialist legal services tailored to businesses, institutions and individuals.

Our team provides comprehensive legal support across a wide range of practice areas. Explore our specialist services below to learn how we can assist you in protecting your interests, resolving disputes and achieving your objectives.

Corporate & Commercial

Commercial Lawyers

Hassan Luk is anchored by seasoned commercial lawyers, each with a history of shaping significant commercial agreements and transactions in leading law firms and legal departments. We bring this depth of expertise to every client — from established businesses to fast-growth start-ups — ensuring robust, commercial legal strategies that drive success.

Our lawyers work across banking, employment, real estate, construction, regulatory compliance and litigation, and are well placed to advise on a wide range of commercial services including procurement, franchising and distribution, advertising agreements, manufacturing and trading, framework services agreements, joint ventures, IP licensing, and B2B and B2C terms and conditions.

Corporate Governance Lawyers

If you are looking to attract senior talent, additional investment and improve stakeholder engagement, a modern corporate governance framework allows you to adapt quickly, mitigate risk and build trust with future shareholders and board committees.

Our corporate advisory team act as your external source of advice, combining regulatory knowledge with a practical understanding of your business needs. We support head office, general counsel and company secretarial teams across company secretarial services, board and committee structures, listing rules compliance, ESG strategy and reporting, internal control frameworks, and crisis management — providing sound legal advice in areas not covered by in-house teams.

Procurement Lawyers

Hassan Luk's specialist procurement lawyers advise both bidders and contracting authorities on challenges arising from procurements across all sectors. The procurement process relies on your ability to identify reliable, cost-effective suppliers and maintain lasting relationships — reducing the risk of delays, defects or disruptions to your business.

Our services span technology procurement, infrastructure equipment, public contracts regulations, business process outsourcing, major projects, and supplier management. Acting as an extension of your team, we provide carefully formulated procurement strategies and ongoing support — whether on a part-time or full-time basis — to ensure compliant and efficient outcomes at any scale.

Dispute Resolution & Arbitration

Arbitration Lawyers

Arbitration is a valuable alternative to court proceedings, particularly where privacy and confidentiality are paramount. When parties choose to arbitrate, they opt for a private dispute resolution procedure governed by agreed rules — offering speed, cost-effectiveness and finality that litigation cannot always provide.

Our arbitration clients include sovereign states, state-owned companies, multi-national corporations, banks, funds and high-net-worth individuals. We support clients from commencement through to enforcement, with experience across the London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA), the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), the Singapore International Arbitration Centre, and UNCITRAL ad hoc proceedings. Our work spans construction, energy, banking and finance, real estate, FinTech, telecommunications, shipping and commodities.

Our services cover the full arbitral process: jurisdictional issues, arbitrator selection, document production, expert witnesses, pleadings and submissions, challenges to the process, costs awards, and enforcement of awards in local and foreign jurisdictions. We also advise on the drafting of arbitration clauses at the contract stage — ensuring you know exactly what to expect should a dispute arise.

Litigation Lawyers

Strategic representation before courts and tribunals in commercial and civil disputes.

Debt Recovery Lawyers

Unpaid debts hold up your business — affecting cash flow, creating uncertainty and potentially harming your reputation. When debtors fail to pay or continue to delay, you need swift and effective action. Our debt recovery lawyers act quickly to protect your commercial interests and recover what you are owed.

Our service covers the full recovery cycle: tracing debtors, letters before action, negotiation, issuing and serving claims, statutory demands, judgment enforcement, and — where necessary — winding up or bankruptcy proceedings. Enforcement methods available include warrants of execution, attachment of earnings orders, charging orders over property, and third-party debt orders.

We operate on a fixed-fee basis for undisputed invoice debt, delivering exceptional value for money. Our clients range from larger businesses with credit managers to individuals and smaller companies who need expert support chasing unpaid bills — whether through the courts or by negotiation. Where a genuine dispute emerges, our commercial litigation specialists step in to handle the matter professionally and efficiently.

Property & Construction

Property Lawyers

Whether you are a property investor buying or selling a single asset or a portfolio, a landlord or tenant entering into a commercial lease, or looking to buy, sell or re-mortgage your residential property, our team has the expertise to guide you through the process with confidence.

We advise across the full spectrum of real estate matters: residential and commercial acquisitions and disposals, real estate finance for lenders and borrowers, landlord and tenant arrangements, real estate litigation, and tax structuring covering stamp duty, capital gains tax, VAT and land rates. Our clients include developers, entrepreneurs, landowners, listed companies, government bodies and major institutions.

Our title investigations are recognised in the market as among the most thorough and detailed available. We work in conjunction with our private client, family, commercial, immigration and dispute resolution teams to structure each transaction in the most efficient way — providing a truly multi-disciplinary service from a single point of contact.

Conveyancing Lawyers

We provide a bespoke and flexible conveyancing service to ensure your sale or purchase is completed thoroughly and as smoothly as possible. From the outset we explain the transfer process clearly, carry out all relevant in-depth searches on your behalf, draft and negotiate the necessary documentation, and report our findings to you comprehensively — minimising the risk to your transaction at every stage.

For residential matters our work ranges from substantial Nairobi houses and luxury penthouses to country estates and modern apartments. For commercial clients we handle hotels, office and apartment blocks, retail parks, restaurants, and industrial and scientific facilities. You will have live access to documents, correspondence and matter updates at all times through our secure digital management tools.

Construction Lawyers

Managing major construction and infrastructure projects requires specialised legal expertise to ensure successful execution and compliance. Our construction lawyers provide comprehensive support across contract drafting and negotiation, risk assessment, regulatory compliance, procurement of equipment, and dispute resolution.

We advise developers, contractors, funders and public authorities on all contractual issues arising during a project's lifecycle — from procurement and planning through to delivery and any disputes that emerge along the way. Our real estate litigation team stands ready to assist where conflicts arise, offering practical and commercial solutions to landlords, occupiers, developers and property managers across retail, office, industrial, leisure and residential sectors.

Family & Private Client

Family Lawyers

When a relationship breaks down, there are many complex issues to resolve — from financial arrangements to the welfare of children. Our family and children team understands how difficult these situations can be, and our advice is always family-centred, practical and responsive, drawing on years of experience to minimise distress to all family members.

We advise on the full range of family matters: divorce and separation, cohabitation agreements, prenuptial agreements, child support and arrangements, adoption and surrogacy, domestic abuse, and international family disputes. Many of our lawyers are collaboratively trained or offer mediation, and we can act for both partners jointly where appropriate — keeping disputes out of court wherever possible, while remaining fully prepared to represent you in litigation when required.

Our children law practitioners have extensive experience resolving disputes sensitively and constructively, with the child's best interests at the centre of everything we do. We also advise on international relocation, child abduction, co-parenting agreements, and the legal aspects of creating families through assisted reproduction and surrogacy.

Divorce Lawyers

Divorce settlements can be complicated and the process overwhelming. We cut through the jargon and offer clear, honest advice on your options — whether you are married or unmarried, and whether the separation is straightforward or involves complex financial and parental arrangements.

Our services cover divorce and civil partnership dissolution, judicial separation agreements, financial settlements, and family mediation. Where children are involved, we advise on maintenance, living arrangements and parental responsibility — focusing on outcomes that work for the whole family. We also offer access to professional counselling support alongside our legal services, recognising that exceptional legal expertise is only one part of our role at such a difficult time.

Succession Lawyers

Dying without a properly drawn will can cause significant wasted time, unnecessary expense and family conflict that could easily be avoided. Our succession lawyers guide you through everything you need to consider — from drafting a will that genuinely reflects your wishes, to reviewing and updating it as your circumstances change, to putting a Lasting Power of Attorney in place should you lose the ability to manage your own affairs.

Where disputes arise, we advise on contesting wills on grounds including lack of testamentary capacity, undue influence, fraud, forgery and rectification claims. Our contentious probate lawyers handle disagreements over estates with clarity and strategy — including international probate matters for clients with assets across multiple jurisdictions. We also offer Sharia-compliant Islamic wills (Wasiyyah) for clients who wish their estate to pass in accordance with the principles of Sharia law.

Hassan Luk also acts as executor for many of our clients, bringing detailed knowledge of the probate process directly into our will-writing service — and offering a seamless, end-to-end succession planning service for individuals and families with both personal and business assets to consider.

Regulatory Compliance

Data Protection Lawyers

Companies and individuals face an increasing number of regulatory requirements, and regulatory bodies are more proactive than ever. Whether you or your organisation is under investigation, undergoing disciplinary proceedings, or managing the reputational damage that may follow, our specialist regulatory lawyers provide a clear path through even the most complex compliance challenges.

Our services span data protection and privacy, financial services regulation with the CBK and PRC, online safety obligations, technology and telecommunications licensing, betting and gaming compliance, political regulation, professional discipline, and corporate governance. We act for both regulated entities facing investigation and for regulatory authorities conducting them — giving us a uniquely rounded understanding of how regulators think, their priorities, and how best to engage with them.

Where enforcement action is taken, our litigators are deeply experienced in public and administrative law, capable of defending the full range of claims through to the highest courts. We also offer business audit and testing, safety management system reviews, and bespoke training packages to drive cultural change and prevent problems before they arise.

FinTech Lawyers

As the FinTech sector continues its rapid growth — from digital finance and artificial intelligence to blockchain, machine learning and payment automation — navigating the complex and ever-changing regulatory landscape is essential to business success. Our senior FinTech lawyers deliver focused, commercially minded advice that mitigates risk and supports growth at every stage, from start-up through to established enterprise.

Our FinTech services cover corporate finance and fundraising, banking and lending platforms, commercial payment agreements (including merchant acquiring, open banking, card scheme arrangements and SaaS contracts), data protection and intellectual property, employee share schemes, and multi-jurisdictional regulatory and payments compliance. Acting as your Virtual General Counsel, we offer senior legal support on a full-time, part-time or flexible basis — tailored to what your business actually needs and can invest.

Employment Lawyers

We advise individuals, employers and partnerships across multiple sectors on both contentious and non-contentious employment matters — from joining through to exit. Our team is a trusted advisor to employers managing workplace challenges, and to senior executives and directors navigating every aspect of their employment relationship. We regularly appear in the Employment and Labour Relations Court and bring strategic depth and creativity to problems at every stage.

Our services cover settlement agreements, salary and exit negotiations, employment contract drafting and staff handbooks, restructuring and redundancy (including TUPE transfers), unfair and constructive dismissal, discrimination, whistleblowing, harassment, and workplace investigations. We also provide bespoke training for organisations on conflict avoidance, performance management and difficult workplace conversations.

Whether you need support on an as-needed basis or want us to operate as an extension of your team for day-to-day employment matters, our model is designed to suit your business — giving you access to expert employment law advice at the right level of engagement for your needs.
