Commercial Law

We understand how businesses work and understand the key business drivers applicable to our clients’ sectors.

Hassan Luk is anchored by seasoned commercial lawyers, each with a history of shaping significant commercial agreements and transactions in leading law firms and legal departments. We bring this depth of law expertise to every client, from big businesses to fast-growth start-ups, ensuring robust and commercial legal strategies that drive success.

We understand how businesses work and understand the key business drivers applicable to our clients’ sectors. Our lawyers are well placed to offer clients suppport across banking, employment, real estate, construction, regulatory compliance and litigation services in a variety of disputes whether contractual, tortious or based in statute.

What commercial legal services we offer?

The procurement process relies on your ability to identify reliable, cost effective suppliers and maintain lasting relationships. Our team of commercial lawyers specialise in all aspects of procurement including contract negotiation, supplier management, and improving procurement processes for your business. Click here to explore procurement page.

Our lawyers help smooth the path to your franchising and distribution success, expertly negotiating contracts and steering clients through regulatory requirements. We’re committed to turning your objectives into achievements with strategic commercial legal support.

Our commercial lawyers are adept at crafting agreements that align with advertising laws and enhance your business’s market position. With our guidance, you can confidently negotiate terms and propel your brand, backed by robust legal compliance.

Hassan Luk's lawyers are experts in drafting and negotiating business manufacturing and trading agreements to help ensure a resilient supply chain and to enhance quality control. We are here to construct a legal framework that supports your business continuity and growth.

A framework services agreement can streamline the handling of intricate details in your business relationship, saving both parties valuable time. With the support of a commercial lawyer at Hassan Luk, you can build long-lasting relationships with trusted suppliers.

At Hassan Luk, we focus on clear legal solutions that support the stability and success of your joint venture. Our commercial lawyers partner with you to develop agreements that serve your strategic objectives and ensure a solid partnership.

We specialise in drafting, reviewing and negotiating intellectual property licence agreements that align with your strategic objectives. As a team, we will work diligently to protect your intellectual property, ensuring that your interests are safeguarded.

Our legal team understands the unique dynamics of business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-consumer (B2C) transactions. We can provide you with robust terms and conditions, helping you to build transparent relationships with businesses and consumers.
