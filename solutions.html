Legal Solutions for Businesses

Our tax experts’ technical expertise is combined with a commercial and responsive approach to all transactions.

Taking on or being chased by KRA can be incredibly stressful. Our experts provide practical advice on any dispute with KRA and have extensive experience supporting, among others, ultra high net worth individuals, corporates charities, and trusts.

We advise on and implement tax effective solutions for individuals and families, including owners of privately held businesses, and high-net-worth individuals with respect to their personal and corporate tax planning needs.

Why our tax lawyers?

Our team has the required experience in negotiating and resolving tax matters with the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), and the expertise to protect your business should the matter escalate to a tribunal or court.

Our relationship with the tax authorities is based on mutual respect. We regularly succeed in settling complex disputes, even in situations where a compromise already seemed out of reach.
